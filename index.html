A city full of good tables, and no good map.

About the journal

Small rooms, long nights, good chalk.

Chalk & Cue is a small independent blog about playing pool around the Greater Toronto Area. Every guide is researched from the venues’ own published hours and rates, then walked through in plain language: how to get there, what it costs, when to show up, and what to expect when you walk in.

What you’ll find here

  • Field guides with addresses, hours, table rates and transit notes for real rooms.
  • Practical detail — reservations, 7ft vs 9ft tables, house rules and etiquette.
  • Routes for turning two or three stops into a proper pool crawl.
  • Honest caveats: rates and hours change. Call ahead before you make the trip.
