About the journal

A notebook with no assigned subject.

Commonplace is a small independent blog for anything worth writing down. The first post happens to be a field guide to pool halls in the Greater Toronto Area, but the journal itself is deliberately general: essays, city notes, how-tos, reading lists and detours.

What you’ll find here

  • Researched guides with real addresses, hours, rates and practical detail.
  • Plain-language notes on how things work, from table etiquette to transit routes.
  • Essays and reading lists whenever the mood strikes.
  • Honest caveats — a source list and a reminder that details change.

Built as a PWA

The journal installs on your phone or desktop, works offline, follows your system light/dark preference, and remembers your reading font and text size. Posts ship with the app, so the whole thing is available on the subway with no signal.
